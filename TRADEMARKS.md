# The LoopCraft name and logo

The code is free under the GNU GPL version 3 (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`).
The name **LoopCraft** and its logo are not: they say where the software
comes from, so users can tell the original from a fork. This page says how
you may use them. It does not limit what the GPL lets you do with the code.

## Always fine

- Talking about LoopCraft: reviews, tutorials, videos, articles, forum posts.
- Saying your software works with LoopCraft, or that a fork is "based on
  LoopCraft".
- Shipping unmodified LoopCraft under its own name (see bundling below).

## Bundling LoopCraft with other software

You may ship unmodified LoopCraft alongside your product, free or paid, as a
separate install (the way audio drivers come with DAWs), and say so, provided
that:

- you describe it as a free, independent project included at no extra
  charge, for example "comes with LoopCraft, a free open-source audio
  router";
- you don't present it as a feature, component or edition of your product,
  and don't use the LoopCraft name or logo in a way that suggests your
  product's price pays for it;
- you show the attribution in `NOTICE` and link to the official project
  page.

## Forks and modified versions

You are welcome to fork LoopCraft and publish your own versions. Give yours
its own name and logo, and say it is based on LoopCraft. Don't call a
modified version LoopCraft or anything that could be mistaken for it (such as
"LoopCraft Pro").

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, ask: open an issue on the project
page.
